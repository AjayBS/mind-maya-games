Mind Maya Games is a game development studio and creator platform built on a simple belief: making games should feel like making art—personal, intentional, and full of meaning.

From solo indie projects to collaborative experiments, we share the creative process through tutorials, devlogs, design breakdowns, and honest glimpses behind the scenes. A space to learn, experiment, and bring ideas to life, one game at a time.
